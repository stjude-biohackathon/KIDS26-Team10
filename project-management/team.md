# Team and Roles

- **Team name:** Team 10 — SQD for Iron–Sulfur Clusters
- **Team lead:** Amandeep Singh Bhatia [deepquantum88](https://github.com/deepquantum88)
- **Communication channel:** [Slack: #team10](https://stjudebiohackathon.slack.com/archives/C0BSA3JQGS1)
- **Project question/problem:** How accurately can sample-based quantum diagonalization (SQD) reproduce the ground-state energy of [2Fe-2S(SMe)₄]²⁻ in a (10-electron, 10-orbital) active space?
- **Expected output:** A reproducible SQD workflow benchmarked against CASCI, with energy convergence, sampling efficiency, and orbital occupancies.
- **Tools and stack:** Python, PySCF, AVAS, ffsim/LUCJ, Qiskit, and qiskit-addon-sqd.
